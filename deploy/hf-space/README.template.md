# SO-101 Assembly Guide in 3D

Every step of the [kitsmith.dev SO-101 guide]({{GUIDE_URL}}/) in 3D, {{SHOWN}} steps across the follower
and leader arms: the step's parts highlighted, the arm built so far beneath them, later parts ghosted.

Each step links to its page on kitsmith.dev, which has the parts, the check, the screws and tools where the
step needs them, and the source for every fact. The servo and gear ratio at every joint, Waveshare part
numbers and Seeed servo numbers mapped to the upstream part names, and troubleshooting pages for motor-setup
errors are there too.
