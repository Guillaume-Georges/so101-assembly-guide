# SO-101 Assembly Guide: 3D preview

A few steps of the [kitsmith.dev SO-101 guide](https://kitsmith.dev/so101/) in 3D: the step's parts
highlighted, the arm built so far beneath them, later parts ghosted.

**The full guide lives at [kitsmith.dev/so101](https://kitsmith.dev/so101/):** 52 steps across the follower
and leader arms, each with its parts and a check, plus the screws and tools where the step needs them; the
servo and gear ratio at every joint;
Waveshare part numbers and Seeed servo numbers mapped to the upstream part names; troubleshooting pages for
motor-setup errors. Every fact links to its public source.

Source and data: [GitHub](https://github.com/Guillaume-Georges/so101-assembly-guide) (MIT).
SO-101 design by [TheRobotStudio](https://github.com/TheRobotStudio/SO-ARM100) (Apache-2.0). Community
